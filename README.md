# Incident Response Tabletop Exercise

> Incident response governance: a cross-functional hybrid cyber and physical threat exercise aligned to the NIST IR lifecycle.

## Objective

Test whether the organization could detect, escalate and respond to a hybrid cyber and physical threat, and fix the coordination gaps before a real incident exposes them.

## Execution

- I designed and led a tabletop exercise around an anonymous threat scenario spanning cyber and physical domains.
- I coordinated participants from the SOC, Physical Security, HR, Legal and Leadership, with decision points tied to the NIST incident response lifecycle.
- I observed escalation paths, decision authority and communication handoffs under pressure.
- I produced an after-action report with lessons learned and specific standard operating procedure (SOP) changes, each with an owner.

## Business outcomes

- Identified gaps in escalation, authority and communication, and drove SOP improvements to close them.
- Clarified who decides and who is informed at each stage, reducing the risk of delayed escalation in a real event.
- Established a repeatable exercise format the organization can run annually to track improvement.

## Skills demonstrated

- Tabletop design and facilitation
- Cross-functional coordination (SOC, HR, Legal, Physical Security, Leadership)
- Incident response governance
- After-action reporting
